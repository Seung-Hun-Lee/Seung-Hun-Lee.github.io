<section class="home-hero">
  <div class="home-hero__text">
    <h1>Seunghun Lee</h1>
    <p class="home-summary home-summary--lead">
      I am an <strong>AI Engineer at Huawei Singapore</strong>. I received my <strong>Ph.D. and B.S. degrees from DGIST</strong> (Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology), where I was advised by Prof. <a href="https://sunghoonim.github.io/">Sunghoon Im</a>.
    </p>
    <p class="home-summary home-summary--research">
      My research trajectory is built on a deep understanding of visual and multimodal systems. During the early stages of my doctoral studies, I focused on <strong>Domain Adaptation</strong>. I subsequently expanded my expertise into <strong>Video Perception</strong>, tackling tasks such as Video Instance Segmentation (VIS), Referring Video Object Segmentation (RVOS), and Moment Retrieval. Currently, my research interests are centered on <strong>Vision-Language-Action (VLA) modeling</strong>, exploring the intersection of multimodal reasoning and actionable intelligence.
    </p>
